Your WESTbahn tickets: what to do

  1. Save the PDF (10 pages: 5 tickets each way) to two phones. Each page is one person on one train; the tickets aren’t in anyone’s name.

The other legs (no booking needed except the trains)

Bus 150 / 150X · Salzburg – St. Gilgen, both ways

Pay the driver or use the Salzburg Verkehr app

  • Out: the first bus 150 after 09:08 from Salzburg Hauptbahnhof, bay F (Südtiroler Platz), 3 min from the platforms. About every 30 min on Sundays, ~49 min to St. Gilgen Busbahnhof.
  • Back: 150X express, St. Gilgen 16:36 → Salzburg Hbf 17:16. Be at the stop by 16:25. Extra buses run on Advent weekends; if it’s full, take the next 150 or a reinforcement bus.
  • ~€6–9 per adult each way; under-6s free. Fold the stroller; the toddler rides on a lap.
  • Check the Sunday times in the Salzburg Verkehr app a week before (the 150X times are from April 2025).

Advent boat · St. Gilgen – St. Wolfgang, both ways

Buy at the pier · Kids free on Advent Sundays

  • Out 11:30 → 12:05 to the SchafbergBahn pier (backup 12:00). Back 15:35 → 16:10 (if that’s Saturday-only, take the 15:05).
  • €11.80 per adult each way, so €94.40 for four, both ways. Children up to 14 free with an adult on Advent Sundays.
  • Some weekend sailings run on Saturdays only: confirm the Sunday times on the pier board.
  • No reservation under 20 people. Online fares: 5schaetze.at.

SchafbergBahn Advent train · Optional

Not in the plan · add it only if everyone feels up to it on the day

  • Cog railway from the St. Wolfgang valley station (right by the pier) to the Schafbergalm (1,364 m), 25 min each way, carriages lit with fairy lights.
  • Sunday trains up: 09:20 · 10:30 · 11:50 · 13:10 · 14:30 · 15:50, plus on-demand extras. Down from the Alm: 11:15 · 12:35 · 13:55 · 14:15 (on-demand) · 15:15 · 16:35.
  • How it fits: up 13:10 → 13:35, Advent on the Alm (music from 13:45), down 14:15 → 14:40 (or 13:55 → 14:20). Not the 15:15: it misses the 15:35 boat. It replaces the slow hour and most of the market time.
  • €50 per adult return (+€200 for the family); children free on Advent Sundays. Buy at the valley station.
  • The Alm is ~5 °C colder (often snowy): toddler snowsuit, carrier. It may stop in snow or wind. Webcam.

The Budapest-trip stations

Wien Hauptbahnhof

1 Dec departure · 4 Dec change

  • Long-distance trains use the raised platforms 3–12. Every platform has lifts.
  • Railjets are long. The screen on the platform shows sectors A–F; wait in the sector for coach 21.
  • ÖBB ticket office (Reisezentrum) in the main hall, 24 h lockers, and BahnhofCity shops with supermarkets, bakeries and a drugstore for nappies and wipes.
  • From the flat: taxi or Uber XL at 10:15, about 10 min, ~€15–25. With 5 bags, take the taxi. With light bags, tram O from Hintere Zollamtsstraße goes direct (~15 min).
  • 4 Dec change (22 min): look for RJX 19981 to Flughafen Wien, 11:42 on the board, then the sector for coach 31. Use lifts only.

Budapest-Keleti

1 Dec arrival · 4 Dec departure

  • A terminus: walk straight off the platform into the great hall, with no stairs.
  • The lower level (lift behind platforms 6–9) has toilets (small fee; carry HUF coins), lockers and the metro.
  • Taxis: official rank, or book a Főtaxi (+36 1 222 2222) or Bolt. Ignore anyone offering a taxi inside the station. To Síp utca 7: 10–15 min, ~HUF 3,000–4,000 per car.
  • Skip the M2 metro with luggage: it isn’t step-free.
  • 4 Dec: the board shows Wien / München and the platform about 20–30 min ahead. Parts of the station are being rebuilt in 2026, so allow for detours.

Budapest-Kelenföld (fallback)

  • Every Vienna train stops here.
  • The M4 metro is step-free: Kelenföld → Kálvin tér ~10 min, then a ~12 min walk to Síp utca. Taxi: 20–25 min.
  • Use it only if your train is held or cut short, or the 08:30 is listed as starting here.

Flughafen Wien (VIE)

  • The station is underground in front of the terminals. Lifts go up to Departures; Emirates uses Terminal 3.
  • First get the tax-free customs stamp (goods and passports ready), then the Emirates bag drop.

On board the Budapest trains

1 Dec · Railjet xpress

  • Modern Austrian train. Family zone in coach 21: kids’ cinema (“Kino”), pram parking, and the WC with baby-changing at the car end.
  • Free Wi-Fi and a socket at every seat, plus a restaurant car.
  • Luggage racks are at the car ends and between seat backs. Board early to get the five bags stowed near your table.
  • Step-free boarding at Wien Hbf.

4 Dec · EuroCity

  • Older, refurbished carriages. Your coach 414 is open-plan (“Grossraumwagen”), seats 101–104 and 108.
  • A few steps up into the train. The two younger adults lift the bags; one adult looks after only the toddler and stroller.
  • Big cases go at the car ends; smaller ones on the overhead racks.
  • Likely no restaurant car: bring a packed egg-free breakfast and water.
  • Toilets on board, but no guaranteed baby-changing, so change the toddler at Keleti first.

Border: Austria still makes spot checks at Hegyeshalom/Nickelsdorf. Keep passports with Schengen visas in a hand bag on both days.
