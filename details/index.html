2DAY
Friday 27 November · Vienna day 1 · overnight Vienna

The imperial heart on foot

ViennaWalking ≈ 4.5 kmSunset 16:00Loop A4 markets

Why this day: it packs most of your must-see list into one compact, flat arc. The two places with day restrictions (the State Hall is closed on Mondays, and KHM needs a timed slot) both fall on today.

  1. 08:00Activate four “7 Tage WIEN” digital tickets in WienMobil (named; carry passports). They cover today through Thursday. Tram 1 from Hintere Zollamtsstraße → Salztorbrücke (3 stops, ~6 min), then ~7 min on foot over the bridge to the Karmelitermarkt.
  2. 08:20Karmelitermarkt: Friday is a farmers’ day. Breakfast at Harvest (vegan, Karmeliterplatz 1) or Zimmer 37 (vegetarian). Check hours
  3. 09:30Walk to St. Stephen’s via Schwedenplatz and Rotenturmstraße (15–18 min).
  4. 09:50St. Stephen’s Cathedral: tourist hours start at 09:00. Interior €8 adult; the North Tower lift to the Pummerin bell is €8 and stroller-friendly (skip the 343-step South Tower). English tour Mon–Fri 10:30. Verified
  5. 10:40Graben & Plague Column → Peterskirche, a gem of baroque gold, open from 08:00, free.
  6. 11:05Demel, Kohlmarkt 14 (opens 10:00, no reservations). Hot chocolate or Melange; the apple strudel’s egg content isn’t stated, so ask. Buy candied violets as gifts. Every cake contains egg. Hours
  7. 11:40Michaelerplatz: Roman ruins, Looshaus, the Michaelertor dome. Then through the Hofburg to Josefsplatz.
  8. 11:55State Hall, Austrian National Library (Fri 09–18; €12 adult, senior €10.50, under-19 free; lift). 45 min. Verified
  9. 12:50Heldenplatz → Burgtor → Maria-Theresien-Platz Christmas village.
  10. 13:15KHM: lunch in the cupola café (kitchen 11:00–16:30; museum ticket required; reserve), then 60–75 min of highlights: the Bruegel room, Kunstkammer, Vermeer. Timed slot + table
  11. 15:30 – 17:15Home to rest: tram 1 from Burgring (outside the KHM) direct to Hintere Zollamtsstraße, ~20 min with no stairs. Or U3 Volkstheater → Landstraße (4 stops) plus the walk.
  12. 17:30Tram 1 from Hintere Zollamtsstraße → Rathausplatz/Burgtheater (direct, ~15 min). Christkindlmarkt as the lights come on. Agree a meeting point at the big tree. Free Advent singing in the Rathaus hall Fri–Sun from 27 Nov, 15:30–19:00 3rd-party.
  13. 19:00Dinner, Café Landtmann, Universitätsring 4. Egg-free: warm apple strudel (A|G), vegan mango cake, vegan pumpkin and sweet-potato soup, beetroot tartare, winter salad. Tram 1 from Rathausplatz/Burgtheater direct to Hintere Zollamtsstraße, home ~20:30. Reserve

3DAY
Saturday 28 November · Parndorf day trip · overnight Vienna

Designer Outlet, then Sacher

ViennaBus 40 min each wayBlack Friday weekendTax-free

Why today: Parndorf is closed on Sundays, and Saturday is the only free day that keeps Sunday for the lake. It falls the day after Black Friday, so expect deep discounts and big crowds. Arrive at opening and do the luxury stores first.

  1. 08:45Leave the flat. Walk ~8 min (or tram O 2 stops) to Landstraße/Wien Mitte, then U4 direct to Karlsplatz (2 stops); exit Oper. ~15–20 min. Step-free alternative: tram 1 from Hintere Zollamtsstraße direct to Oper/Karlsplatz, the long way round the Ring (~25 min).
  2. 09:30Blaguss shopping shuttle from Opernring 3, opposite the State Opera. About 40 min. Return fare €26 adult, €13 child 3–6, under-3 free (older info). Non-refundable; times fixed. Timetable Book
  3. 10:10 – 14:15McArthurGlen Designer Outlet Parndorf (Sat 09–18): Prada, Gucci, Burberry, BOSS, Michael Kors, Polo Ralph Lauren, Moncler/Armani (check the current directory). Then the Parndorf Fashion Outlet next door for mid-market brands and its “Christmas Home”. Stroller and Kiddie Cab at Guest Services; bag drop €5.
  4. 12:30Lunch on site: Amici Napoli (margherita and marinara pizza dough is normally egg-free, but ask), Green District bowls, wagamama vegan menu (avoid egg noodles). Sacher Café on site for coffee.
  5. 14:30Shuttle back (or 16:00 if shopping is the priority) → Opera ~15:10.
  6. 15:15Café Sacher, Philharmoniker Str. 4. Egg-free options: the vegan white-chocolate and blueberry tartlet, vegan schnitzel, wild-mushroom gnocchi (vegan option), burrata. The Original Sacher-Torte contains egg. Reserve (Aleno)
  7. 16:30Choose: home to rest and drop the bags, or U1 Karlsplatz → Praterstern (direct, 8 min) for the Prater Wintermarkt and a night ride on the Riesenrad (open all year; under-3 free), then tram O from Praterstern → Hintere Zollamtsstraße (3 stops), home ~19:00.
  8. EveningCook a packed lunch for tomorrow in the apartment kitchen (parathas, theplas, fruit) and fill a flask.

4DAY
Sunday 29 November · 1st Advent · Wolfgangsee day trip · overnight Vienna

Lake, boat and Advent lights

Salzkammergut~4 h travel each waySunset ≈ 16:20Top 5 · #1SchafbergBahn optional

Why this day works, and the honest cost: the Wolfgangseer Advent is open this weekend, and on Advent Sundays children ride the boats and the Schafbergbahn for free. It is still about 8 hours of travel in a 14-hour day, so the plan uses the fast train, then the regional bus with no luggage to carry, and loops the lake by boat (St. Gilgen → St. Wolfgang and back) so the bus leaves from where it dropped you.

  1. 06:05Leave the flat. At this hour a taxi is easiest (~15 min, ~€15–20). Or take tram O from Hintere Zollamtsstraße, 2 stops to Landstraße/Wien Mitte, then the U3 direct to Westbahnhof (~25 min; check the first tram O in WienMobil). The train is usually ready ~30 min before departure, so board by ~06:25. Packed breakfast.
  2. 06:38 → 09:08WESTbahn Wien Westbahnhof → Salzburg Hbf (train 902). Booked 17 Oct: coach 15, upper deck, seats 524A, 524B, 525A, 525B, 525C (toddler has their own seat). €73.96 (WestSuperpreis €13.99 + 3 × €18.99, toddler €3). Cancellable online until the day before (25%, minimum €4.99 per adult ticket). The family compartment with the changing table is downstairs in coach 14. Booked Scenic side: LEFT (south) for the Alpine foothills from Linz to Salzburg (light from ~07:45).
  3. 09:15Bus to St. Gilgen from the stop right outside the station: Salzburg Hauptbahnhof, bay F (Südtiroler Platz), 3 min walk. Take the first bus 150 after you arrive (about every 30 min on Sundays, ~49 min). The early start beats the late-morning crowds, and Hbf is the first stop, so you’ll get seats. Fold the stroller. Tickets from the driver or the Salzburg Verkehr app, ~€6–9 per adult, under-6s free. Fare estimate
  4. ~10:05 – 11:25St. Gilgen: a short walk (~8 min) from the Busbahnhof to the pier through the village. The market opens at 10:00, so you’ll have it almost to yourselves: the 11 m Advent candle on the lake shore, the nativity, coffee and a warm-up before the boat. You’ll pass through again at dusk.
  5. 11:30 → 12:05Advent boat St. Gilgen → St. Wolfgang (35 min), docking at the Schafbergbahn pier, right beside the mountain-railway station. €11.80 per adult one way; children up to 14 free on Advent Sundays. Buy at the St. Gilgen pier. Some weekend boats run on Saturdays only, so confirm the Sunday times at the pier; the 12:00 (→ 12:35) is the backup. 2026 timetable
  6. 12:10 – 13:00Warm lunch indoors at Café EQ in the ErlebnisQuartier (the SchafbergBahn valley station, beside the pier): indoor Advent market and cookie decorating, plus your packed food. WC and nappy change.
  7. 13:00 – 13:45Slow hour: toddler nap in the stroller or carrier while the grandparents stay warm in the café, or a gentle walk along the lakeside promenade past the floating 16 m Friedenslicht lantern.
  8. 13:45 – 15:20St. Wolfgang market lanes (about a 10-min walk) and the pilgrimage church (Pacher altar), at an easy pace with a hot-drink stop, then back to the SchafbergBahn pier.
  9. 15:35 → 16:10Boat back to St. Gilgen as the lake lights come on, your second crossing. If the 15:35 is a Saturday-only sailing, take the 15:05 (→ 15:40). Don’t plan on the 16:10: it arrives too late for the bus.
  10. 16:10 – 16:25St. Gilgen market at dusk on the way to the bus station.

Tickets: at the valley station on arrival (~12:10): €50 per adult return to the Schafbergalm, children free on Advent Sundays, under-4s free without a seat (or online if the Advent fare is offered). Family +€200. Sunday trains up (25 min): 09:20 · 10:30 · 11:50 · 13:10 · 14:30 · 15:50, plus on-demand extras. Down from the Alm: 11:15 · 12:35 · 13:55 · 14:15 (on-demand) · 15:15 · 16:35. Optional ride
A cog railway from the St. Wolfgang valley station (542 m) to the middle station Schafbergalm (1,364 m), 25 min each way. In Advent it doesn’t go to the summit. The carriages are enclosed but dress as if they’re unheated. Trains can be cancelled in snowfall or bad weather. Live webcam: schafbergalm.panomax.com.

No luggage, the toddler rides free, and it saves ~€150–250 over a van. The trade-offs: buses can be crowded on Advent Sundays (possibly standing in the morning), a fixed boat back at 15:35, and the toddler on a lap instead of in a car seat.

Health
Hot mineral water is demanding: 15–20 min per hot pool, then rest and drink water. No alcohol before or during. Get out if you feel dizzy. If either of you is pregnant, or has heart or blood-pressure problems, ask a doctor before going.
Etiquette
Mixed bathing in swimwear everywhere. Shower before the pools. Don’t photograph other bathers. Saunas are quiet zones.
Food
The bath’s buffet has little reliable vegetarian food. Eat at Govinda first and carry fruit or nuts for afterwards.
Scams
Ignore anyone selling tickets outside the gate. Buy only on the official site or at the bath’s own cashier.

Euro, forint, data and refunds

Money, SIM & tax-free

Euro & forint

  • Euro cash: carry about €300–500 from India, for markets, cup deposits and WCs. Check the current RBI cash limit with your forex dealer.
  • Main card: a zero-markup card (e.g. Niyo Global) plus one backup credit card. BookMyForex cards charge 3.5 % when a euro balance is spent in forint.
  • Forint: don’t buy it in India. Withdraw HUF 20,000–40,000 at a bank ATM (OTP, K&H, Erste). Avoid Euronet ATMs.
  • Always choose to be charged in EUR/HUF, never INR (decline the conversion offer at the terminal).

SIM & eSIM

  • Recommended: Airalo Europe unlimited, 10 days (~$35), on two phones. Install in India, switch on at landing. 16 Oct
  • Grandparents: an Indian roaming pack (Airtel ~₹2,997 / Jio ~₹2,799 for 10 days Check app), or Wi-Fi + a hotspot from a family phone.
  • Cheapest: HoT “smart” €5.90 / 10 GB from a HOFER supermarket in Vienna (passport registration; small surcharge in Hungary).
  • Keep Indian SIMs active for bank OTPs (receiving SMS is usually free).

Tax-free & tips

  • Austrian purchases ≥ €75.01 per store per day: ask for a tax-free form with your passport. Get it stamped at VIE customs before check-in on 4 Dec, with the goods unused.
  • Tipping in Austria: round up 5–10 % and say the total when paying.
  • Tipping in Hungary: if the bill shows szervízdíj (service charge, 10–15 %), don’t tip again; otherwise leave 10 %.
