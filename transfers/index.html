03

How to book

Before you book

  1. Vienna flat: 5th floor with a lift, self check-in. The host sends the details on 26 Nov. Have mobile data working when you land so the message arrives, and screenshot the instructions before you leave the airport. Use the house number from your Booking.com confirmation on rides A, B, E and F.
  2. Budapest flat: the booking still says “arrival between 10:00 and 11:00” and 4 adults. Message Fusion Apartments through Booking.com: you arrive around 15:05 from Keleti, and please add the toddler and a cot.

Enter the same details on every booking

Where
welcomepickups.com or the Welcome Pickups app. Use one account for all six rides. Backup if they cannot confirm the seat: Sixt Ride, XL class.
Vehicle
Minivan, 8 seats (Mercedes V-Class or Vito long, or similar). With five people seated, the spare third-row seats take the overflow bags.
Passengers
5 (4 adults, 1 child). Count the toddler, because the seat takes a place.
Luggage
Rides A to D: 6 large suitcases + 1 folding stroller. Enter all six as large and ask the operator to confirm in writing that they fit. If not, upgrade that ride to a minibus rather than splitting the family across two cars. Rides E and F: no suitcases, only the stroller and day bags. Still book a minivan, because five people plus a child seat will not fit a sedan.
Child seat
1 × child seat for 9–18 kg. In the note, say “18 months, 10 kg”. Not a booster. An infant carrier (up to 13 kg) also works at 10 kg if the head stays below the top of the shell.
Name sign
MITTAL, with the lead name as on your bookings
Phone
An Indian mobile with WhatsApp. Airalo eSIMs are usually data-only with no number, so drivers will reach you on WhatsApp over data.
Before paying
Read the cancellation terms and any child-seat charge on the checkout screen, then save each confirmation to two phones.

Airport arrival, Vienna

Drop-off, about20:05Vienna flatKolonitzgasse, 1030 Wien (Landstraße). House number in your Booking.com confirmation.

Your flight
EK125 leaves Dubai T3 at 15:40, lands Vienna T3 at 18:50. Enter the flight number so the driver tracks it.
Why 19:30
Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting from the pickup time. Starting at 19:30 covers the non-EU passport queue and six bags until 20:30.
Meeting point
Arrivals hall, driver holding a MITTAL sign
At the flat
5th floor with a lift, self check-in (details sent on the day, open until 23:00). Older Vienna lifts are often small, so send the grandparents and toddler up first with one adult, then bring the six cases up in two or three trips. Dinner: Indus, Radetzkystraße 20, about 300 m away.
Best way
Pre-booked minivan. The CAT bus (€14.90 per adult) stops 600 m from the flat, which is too far at night with six cases and a toddler. A taxi from the rank may not have a child seat or room for the bags.

Note for the driver

Family of 5: 4 adults and an 18-month-old (10 kg). Please bring a child seat for 9–18 kg. Luggage: 6 large suitcases and a folding stroller. We arrive on EK125 from Dubai with non-EU passports, so passport control may take a while. Please wait in the arrivals hall with a name sign: MITTAL.

If it goes wrong: message the driver from the app. With no van at all, use the taxi rank outside arrivals and ask for a Großraumtaxi (large taxi), or take the CAT bus to Marxergasse (4 × €14.90, toddler free).

E · Sun 29 Nov

Flat to Westbahnhof, Wolfgangsee day
Pickup06:05Vienna flat, at the street doorKolonitzgasse, 1030 Wien
Drop-off, about06:20Wien Westbahnhof, main entranceEuropaplatz 2, 1150 Wien
6.6 km by road12–15 min on quiet Sunday roads€40–60 estimate
Your train
WESTbahn 902 leaves Wien Westbahnhof at 06:38, arrives Salzburg Hbf at 09:08. Coach 15, upper deck. Board by about 06:25.
Why 06:05
You reach the station about 18 minutes before departure, enough to find coach 15 and get upstairs with the stroller folded. Address pickups get only 15 minutes of free waiting, so be downstairs at 06:00. Few drivers are about at 06:00 on a Sunday, which is the main reason to pre-book this one.
Luggage
No suitcases. Folding stroller, day bags, toddler snowsuit.
Best way
Pre-booked minivan. The U3 runs direct from Landstraße (Wien Mitte) to Westbahnhof in about 15 minutes, and your Vienna travel cards cover it. But it means a 10-minute walk in the dark at about 0 °C with grandparents and a toddler, and less margin for the train.

Note for the driver

Early Sunday pickup for the 06:38 WESTbahn from Wien Westbahnhof, so please be on time. Family of 5 with an 18-month-old (10 kg): child seat for 9–18 kg needed. No suitcases, only a folding stroller and day bags. Drop-off at the Westbahnhof main entrance on Europaplatz, please.

If it goes wrong: no car by 06:12, call Taxi 40100 on +43 1 40100 for a Großraumtaxi or order Uber XL. Last resort: walk to Landstraße (Wien Mitte) and take the U3 direct to Westbahnhof.
