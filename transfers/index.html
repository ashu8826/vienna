Your trains
WESTbahn 902 leaves Wien Westbahnhof at 06:38 (coach 15, upper deck). WESTbahn 927 brings you back, arriving 20:22.
Morning
  1. 05:35 Lift down from the 5th floor and walk about 650 m to Landstraße (Wien Mitte) U-Bahn station, about 10 minutes with the stroller.
  2. ~05:50 Lift down to the U3 platform. Take the U3 towards Ottakring.
  3. Seven stops, about 12 minutes: Stubentor, Stephansplatz, Herrengasse, Volkstheater, Neubaugasse, Zieglergasse, Westbahnhof. Early on a Sunday the U3 runs about every 10–15 minutes.
  4. ~06:10 At Westbahnhof, follow the signs to the station hall. There is a direct step-free link from the U3.
  5. 06:20 On the WESTbahn platform at coach 15. Fold the stroller and go up to the upper deck.
Evening
  1. 20:22 Off WESTbahn 927. Come down from the upper deck and follow the U3 signs to the lift.
  2. Take the U3 towards Simmering, seven stops to Landstraße (Wien Mitte), about 12 minutes. On Sunday evening trains run about every 7–10 minutes.
  3. Walk about 650 m home. Home around 21:00.
Why 05:35
At this hour you may wait up to 15 minutes for a U3. Leaving at 05:35 still puts you on the platform by 06:20 if you just miss one. On Saturday evening, check the exact U3 times in WienMobil and aim for a train that reaches Westbahnhof by 06:15.
With a toddler
Use the lifts, not the escalators, with the stroller and the grandparents. All Vienna U-Bahn stations have lifts; at Westbahnhof they are being renewed during 2026, but at least one of each pair stays in service. It is dark and around 0 °C at 05:35: snowsuit and stroller cover.
Tickets
Your four 7-day Vienna cards (valid from 27 Nov) cover the U-Bahn. Children under 6 ride free. Open the tickets in WienMobil before you leave the flat.

If it goes wrong: if you miss the U3 or a lift is out of order, call Taxi 40100 on +43 1 40100 or order Uber XL to Westbahnhof (about 12 minutes; no child seat guaranteed). In the evening, if the toddler is asleep or everyone is tired, take a large taxi from the rank on Europaplatz.
