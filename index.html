Before you go

All times are local. Sightseeing times are suggestions; flights and trains are the booked schedule. Recheck operators’ apps for changes.

4 December: Budapest → Wien Hbf → Vienna Airport is booked, but the 22-minute change at Wien Hbf and the flight connection leave little room for disruption. See the rail companion and arrange a practical backup before departure.

Vienna

Austria · euro (€) · German
Base: Landstraße, 26 Nov – 1 Dec

Trams, U-Bahn and taxis. Airport and station rides are booked; times and the metro route for 29 Nov are on the transfers page.

Budapest

Hungary · forint (HUF) · Hungarian
Base: central Pest, 1 – 4 Dec

Metro, trams and taxis. Both station rides are booked (see the transfers page). The M2 metro is not step-free.

02

Friday 27 November Vienna

The imperial heart

  1. 09:00
    Old town at an easy pace

    St. Stephen’s Cathedral, Graben and Kohlmarkt; stop at Demel for a warm drink.

  2. Midday
    Hofburg and museum quarter

    Choose the National Library’s State Hall or the Kunsthistorisches Museum, not both if the family is tired. Lunch nearby.

  3. Afternoon
    Return to the apartment

    Time for the toddler’s nap and a proper rest.

  4. Dusk
    Rathausplatz market and dinner

    Enjoy the lights; Café Landtmann is an option if reserved and suitable food is confirmed.

Tip Use the tram or a taxi between stops; the old-town loop is flexible.

Saturday 28 November Vienna · day trip

Parndorf, then café time

  1. Morning
    Designer Outlet Parndorf

    Shopping shuttle from central Vienna; check times and book if taking it. Leave space for breakfast and a child-friendly break.

  2. Afternoon
    Return to Vienna

    Rest at the apartment. Café Sacher is an optional treat; ask for an egg-free choice.

  3. Evening
    Early dinner and pack for tomorrow

    Bring warm layers, snacks and the train tickets for the lake excursion.

Alternative Skip shopping for a slower Vienna day if the family needs it.

04

Sunday 29 November Wolfgangsee

Lake, boat & Advent lights

  1. 05:35
    U3 metro to Westbahnhof

    Walk 650 m to Landstraße (Wien Mitte), then the U3 towards Ottakring, 7 stops. No ride booked; route on the transfers page.

  2. 06:38
    WESTbahn 902 · Vienna → Salzburg

    Wien Westbahnhof → Salzburg Hbf 09:08. Booked; seats are on the upper deck.

  3. Morning
    Bus 150 to St. Gilgen

    Check Sunday bus times and allow for a possible missed connection; enjoy the village and lakeside market.

  4. Midday
    Boat to St. Wolfgang

    Confirm the Advent sailing schedule in advance. Lunch indoors, then a slow market walk and toddler nap.

  5. Afternoon
    Return by boat and bus

    Choose connections that reach Salzburg Hbf comfortably before the booked train. Weather and full buses can disrupt the plan.

  6. 17:52
    WESTbahn 927 · Salzburg → Vienna

    Arrives Wien Westbahnhof 20:22. Have supper or snacks on the train, then the U3 towards Simmering to Landstraße; home about 21:00.

Honest pace This is a long, early day with roughly four hours’ travel each way. If it feels too much, choose a relaxed Vienna day instead; check ticket conditions before changing plans.

07

Wednesday 2 December Budapest

Parliament & the river

  1. Morning
    Parliament and the Danube

    View Parliament from outside, then visit the Shoes on the Danube memorial. A guided interior tour is optional and needs a ticket.

  2. Midday
    St. Stephen’s Basilica and lunch

    Take a short city-centre walk; Indigo or another vegetarian-friendly restaurant is an option.

  3. Afternoon
    Rest at the apartment

    Allow enough time for a warm break and nap.

  4. Dusk
    Optional Danube cruise

    See the illuminated riverfront, then visit a Christmas market if open. Check departure, weather and toddler arrangements before booking.

Dinner Napfényes is a vegan option; check opening hours and reserve if needed.

08

Thursday 3 December Budapest

Castle views & a gentle farewell

  1. Morning
    Buda Castle district

    Fisherman’s Bastion and Matthias Church; take a taxi up to save energy and avoid steep streets with the stroller.

  2. Midday
    Lunch and Market Hall

    Govinda is a vegetarian option. Make the market optional if the morning has been full.

  3. Afternoon
    Rest, or an optional adults-only bath

    At Széchenyi, check current age and admission rules: the toddler should not be assumed eligible. Grandparents and toddler can rest at the apartment.

  4. Evening
    Early dinner and pack

    Check train running information; the 07:30 ride to Keleti is booked.

Tomorrow The return flight leaves from Vienna, not Budapest.

06

Food & family notes

Eating well

Ask about egg, meat stock, fish sauce, gelatine and lard. Vegan dishes can be useful, but confirm ingredients and preparation. Many classic cakes contain egg.

Vienna: Swing Kitchen, Velani, Indus and Max & Benito are options.
Budapest: Napfényes, Govinda and Indigo are options. Check hours and menus.

Show this when ordering

German
Wir essen kein Fleisch, keinen Fisch und keine Eier. Milchprodukte sind okay.

Hungarian
Nem eszünk húst, halat és tojást. A tejtermék rendben van.

Winter kit: warm layers, waterproof shoes, hats and gloves, stroller cover, toddler blanket, nappies and snacks. Carry passports and tickets securely; keep a copy offline. Daylight is short, especially at the lake.
