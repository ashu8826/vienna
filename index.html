Friday 27 November Vienna

The imperial heart

  1. 09:00
    Old town at an easy pace

    St. Stephen’s Cathedral, Graben and Kohlmarkt; stop at Demel for a warm drink.

  2. Midday
    Hofburg and museum quarter

    Choose the National Library’s State Hall or the Kunsthistorisches Museum, not both if the family is tired. Lunch nearby.

  3. Afternoon
    Return to the apartment

    Time for the toddler’s nap and a proper rest.

  4. Dusk
    Rathausplatz market and dinner

    Enjoy the lights; Café Landtmann is an option if reserved and suitable food is confirmed.

Tip Use the tram or a taxi between stops; the old-town loop is flexible.

  2. Afternoon

  3. Evening

Alternative Skip shopping for a slower Vienna day if the family needs it.

Sunday 29 November Wolfgangsee

Lake, boat & Advent lights

  1. 06:38
    WESTbahn 902 · Vienna → Salzburg

    Wien Westbahnhof → Salzburg Hbf 09:08. Booked; seats are on the upper deck.

  2. Morning
    Bus 150 to St. Gilgen

    Check Sunday bus times and allow for a possible missed connection; enjoy the village and lakeside market.

  3. Midday
    Boat to St. Wolfgang

    Confirm the Advent sailing schedule in advance. Lunch indoors, then a slow market walk and toddler nap.

  4. Afternoon
    Return by boat and bus

    Choose connections that reach Salzburg Hbf comfortably before the booked train. Weather and full buses can disrupt the plan.

  5. 17:52

Honest pace This is a long, early day with roughly four hours’ travel each way. If it feels too much, choose a relaxed Vienna day instead; check ticket conditions before changing plans.

Wednesday 2 December Budapest

Parliament & the river

    Parliament and the Danube

    View Parliament from outside, then visit the Shoes on the Danube memorial. A guided interior tour is optional and needs a ticket.

  2. Midday
    St. Stephen’s Basilica and lunch

  3. Afternoon

    Allow enough time for a warm break and nap.

Thursday 3 December Budapest

Castle views & a gentle farewell

    Buda Castle district

    Check train running information and arrange the morning transfer to Keleti.

Tomorrow The return flight leaves from Vienna, not Budapest.

04

Things to arrange

  • Ask the Vienna host about exact address, floor and lift, sleeping arrangements, cot, arrival time and check-in procedure.
  • Confirm the Budapest reservation, toddler and cot, arrival details and cancellation terms in Booking.com.
  • Arrange the Vienna arrival transfer and both station transfers with space for luggage and an appropriate child seat.
  • Print cross-border ÖBB tickets as instructed on the ticket; save both rail PDFs offline and check live schedules close to departure.
  • Plan a realistic 4 December delay contingency; reconfirm the Keleti pickup and airline check-in deadlines.
  • Recheck Parndorf shuttle, Wolfgangsee buses and boat, market opening dates, restaurant hours and any attraction tickets before committing.
  • Confirm egg-free airline meals, travel insurance, roaming and the stroller rules for each flight.

Food & family notes

Eating well

Ask about egg, meat stock, fish sauce, gelatine and lard. Vegan dishes can be useful, but confirm ingredients and preparation. Many classic cakes contain egg.

Vienna: Swing Kitchen, Velani, Indus and Max & Benito are options.
Budapest: Napfényes, Govinda and Indigo are options. Check hours and menus.

Show this when ordering

German
Wir essen kein Fleisch, keinen Fisch und keine Eier. Milchprodukte sind okay.

Hungarian
Nem eszünk húst, halat és tojást. A tejtermék rendben van.

Winter kit: warm layers, waterproof shoes, hats and gloves, stroller cover, toddler blanket, nappies and snacks. Carry passports and tickets securely; keep a copy offline. Daylight is short, especially at the lake.
