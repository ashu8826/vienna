The Inner-Stadt walking route

If you only remember five things

Top five experiences

  1. The Wolfgangsee Advent by boat
    Sun 29 Nov, first Sunday of Advent: St. Gilgen’s lakeside market, a boat across the lake to St. Wolfgang, and the lights coming on at 16:15. Open 29 Nov
  2. The imperial heart on foot, ending under the KHM cupola
    Fri 27 Nov: St. Stephen’s, Graben, Demel, Michaelerplatz, the baroque State Hall, then lunch in the domed café of the Kunsthistorisches Museum. Book KHM slot
  3. Rathausplatz by lamplight, then Café Landtmann
    Fri 27 Nov: Vienna’s biggest market at dusk, then egg-free apple strudel across the road in Freud’s old café. Reserve
  4. Pandas, polar bears and the palace market
    Mon 30 Nov: Schönbrunn Zoo, the world’s oldest, is flat and stroller-friendly, and it’s free for under-6s. Then the Schönbrunn Christmas market. Hours
  5. Budapest from the water
    Wed 2 Dec: a one-hour Danube Legend cruise at blue hour past the lit Parliament, then Vörösmarty tér. Schedule Book

Deliberately left out: Schönbrunn Palace interiors and the Upper Belvedere (Belvedere is the Sunday Plan B), Hallstatt, late concerts, and the Széchenyi Baths for the family (under-14s aren’t admitted).

The imperial heart on foot

ViennaWalking ≈ 4.5 kmSunset 16:00Loop A4 markets

Why this day: it packs most of your must-see list into one compact, flat arc. The two places with day restrictions (the State Hall is closed on Mondays, and KHM needs a timed slot) both fall on today.

  1. 08:00Activate four “7 Tage WIEN” digital tickets in WienMobil (named; carry passports). They cover today through Thursday. Tram 1 from Hetzgasse → Schwedenplatz (~6 min), then 8 min over the Salztorbrücke.

Designer Outlet, then Sacher

ViennaBus 40 min each wayBlack Friday weekendTax-free

Why today: Parndorf is closed on Sundays, and Saturday is the only free day that keeps Sunday for the lake. It falls the day after Black Friday, so expect deep discounts and big crowds. Arrive at opening and do the luxury stores first.

  2. 09:30Blaguss shopping shuttle from Opernring 3, opposite the State Opera. About 40 min. Return fare €26 adult, €13 child 3–6, under-3 free (older info). Non-refundable; times fixed. Timetable Book

Money, SIM & tax-free

Euro & forint

  • Euro cash: carry about €300–500 from India, for markets, cup deposits and WCs. Check the current RBI cash limit with your forex dealer.
  • Main card: a zero-markup card (e.g. Niyo Global) plus one backup credit card. BookMyForex cards charge 3.5 % when a euro balance is spent in forint.
  • Forint: don’t buy it in India. Withdraw HUF 20,000–40,000 at a bank ATM (OTP, K&H, Erste). Avoid Euronet ATMs.
  • Always choose to be charged in EUR/HUF, never INR (decline the conversion offer at the terminal).

SIM & eSIM

  • Recommended: Airalo Europe unlimited, 10 days (~$35), on two phones. Install in India, switch on at landing. 16 Oct
  • Grandparents: an Indian roaming pack (Airtel ~₹2,997 / Jio ~₹2,799 for 10 days Check app), or Wi-Fi + a hotspot from a family phone.

Tax-free & tips

  • Austrian purchases ≥ €75.01 per store per day: ask for a tax-free form with your passport. Get it stamped at VIE customs before check-in on 4 Dec, with the goods unused.
